# Air Pollution Mapping and Service Development Using High-Resolution Satellite Imagery and Ground Observations 🌍

## Project Overview

Air pollution poses serious risks to the environment and public health. However, estimating particulate matter concentrations in areas without ground monitoring stations remains challenging.

This project aims to develop **machine learning-based air pollution prediction models** using **Sentinel-2 satellite imagery** and **AirKorea air quality data**, and to provide a **high-resolution mapping service** that visualizes particulate matter concentrations.

---

## Key Features

- **Air Pollution Prediction Models**
  - Develop high-resolution predictions using machine learning algorithms such as Random Forest (RF), LightGBM (LGBM), XGBoost, and deep neural networks (DNNs).
  - Explore the potential to estimate air pollution in regions with limited ground observations, including North Korea. Prediction reliability in these regions requires further validation.

- **Particulate Matter Map Visualization**
  - Present air quality conditions on maps through a user-friendly interface.
  - Support potential applications in environmental management and policy planning.

- **Potential for Future Expansion**
  - Explore extending air quality predictions across the Korean Peninsula, including North Korea, and eventually to other regions worldwide.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Data Sources**
  - Sentinel-2 satellite imagery
  - AirKorea air quality data
  - Digital Elevation Model (DEM) and meteorological data

- **Machine Learning and Data Analysis**
  - Python: `scikit-learn`, `XGBoost`, `LightGBM`, `TensorFlow`
  - Data preprocessing and missing-value handling

- **Result Visualization**
  - GIS and air quality mapping tools
  - Python: `matplotlib`, `seaborn`

---

## Expected Impact

- **Improved Access to Air Pollution Information**
  - Help individuals and public agencies make more informed decisions about air quality management and policy planning.

- **Applications in Data-Sparse Regions**
  - Investigate approaches that could be adapted to regions with limited monitoring data, including North Korea and other areas worldwide.

- **Contributions to Environmental Sustainability**
  - Support air pollution mitigation and urban planning through more accessible spatial air quality information.
